Tucumán, Argentina

BROD is a DJ and producer focused on a dark, hypnotic and rhythmic sound, where groove is the main axis of every set and production.

His proposal combines the strength of techno with influences from techhouse, breakbeat and hardgroove, seeking to generate a physical and emotional experience on the floor.
